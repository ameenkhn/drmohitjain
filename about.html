About The Practice

Think hard and focus on the patient's well-being.

Dr. Mohit Jain (MD Paediatrics, MD Physician) is a Senior Child Specialist & Allergist with over 28 years of experience, leading Dr Jain Mother & Child Clinic in Sector 14, Noida. Trained in allergy testing & immunotherapy, he is a member of the IMA, IAP, IAP Allergy & Immunology Chapter, ICAAAI and EAACI. His practice is built on careful listening, sound clinical science, and gentle, reassuring care for children at every stage of growth.

From a newborn's first check-up to vaccinations, asthma, and complex allergies, every child is seen as an individual. Parents are kept informed and involved throughout, so that diagnosis and treatment always feel clear, calm, and child-friendly.

Child-centred care Growth, nutrition, immunity, and development are looked at together, with patience and warmth at every visit.
Complete vaccination Well baby clinics and immunisation for children, adolescents, and the elderly, using the best-quality vaccines.
Allergy & asthma expertise Accurate diagnosis through allergy testing, skin prick tests, and molecular diagnostics, followed by tailored treatment.

Core philosophy

Every child deserves to feel safe and unhurried.
Symptoms are signals, not enemies.
Care is a partnership with parents.
Prevention through timely vaccination matters most.
A breathing child should never struggle alone.
Think hard and focus on the patient's well-being.

How the clinic thinks

Consultations look beyond the immediate complaint to understand the whole child: growth and development, immunity, nutrition, environment, and family history. The aim is to treat the cause, not just the symptom.

Care across childhood

The clinic supports children through paediatric consultations, well baby clinics, complete vaccination for children, adolescents, and the elderly, and ongoing follow-up as your child grows.

Allergy & asthma depth

For asthma and allergic conditions, Dr. Jain combines clinical assessment with allergy testing, skin prick tests, immunotherapy, and component or molecular diagnostics for asthma and allergic rhinitis.

Areas of focus in practice

The clinic brings together everyday paediatric care and specialist expertise in allergy and asthma. From routine consultations and immunisation to the diagnosis and long-term management of allergic conditions, the focus is always on keeping your child healthy, comfortable, and thriving.
